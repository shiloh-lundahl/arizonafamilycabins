Licano's Mexican Food & Steakhouse sits on West Deuce of Clubs in Show Low, Arizona, and it's been feeding people in the White Mountains for a long time. This isn't a restaurant chasing a trend — it's a long-running, locally owned spot that's built its reputation one plate at a time, and around here that kind of staying power means something.

If you're staying at either of our cabins in Lakeside and need a reason to head into Show Low, Licano's gives you a good one.

## What to Know About Licano's

Licano's covers two things well: traditional Mexican food and steakhouse fare, which is a combination you don't run into everywhere. That range means a big group with different tastes — someone wanting a classic Mexican plate, someone else in the mood for a steak — can usually find something at the same table without a debate over where to eat.

The dish worth calling out specifically is the Navajo taco. If you haven't had one, it's a regional specialty across the White Mountains and reservation-adjacent areas of Arizona — frybread, that pillowy, golden fried dough, used as the base instead of a tortilla, then loaded up with taco toppings. It's a hearty, filling dish and one of those foods that's genuinely tied to this part of the state rather than something you'd find at a generic Mexican restaurant anywhere. If you've never tried a Navajo taco, Licano's is a solid place to check that box.

## Hours and Planning Ahead

Licano's is open daily from 11am to 9:30pm, but it's closed on Tuesdays — so if you're planning a supply run and a meal around a specific day, keep that in mind and route around Tuesday if you can. Showing up on the one day it's closed is an easy mistake to make when you're juggling a whole trip itinerary, so it's worth double-checking before you build your day around it.

As with most local restaurants, it's always smart to call ahead if you're bringing a larger group, just so they can get you seated without a long wait.
