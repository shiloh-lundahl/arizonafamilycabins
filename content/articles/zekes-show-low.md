## What to Know About Zeke's

Show Low earned its name from a card game — the story goes that the winning hand "showed low," and the town's main drag, Deuce of Clubs, is named for the card that clinched it. That bit of frontier character carries into the local dining scene, where the places that stick around are the ones locals actually keep coming back to. Zeke's is one of those spots.

Rather than promise you a specific dish, here's the honest version: it's a well-regarded local eatery with the kind of reputation you can only build by feeding your neighbors well, year after year. When you're in an unfamiliar town, "the locals eat here" is exactly the signal you want.
