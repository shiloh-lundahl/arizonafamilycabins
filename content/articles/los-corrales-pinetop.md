Los Corrales Family Mexican Restaurant sits on White Mountain Boulevard in Pinetop, and it's the kind of place that shows up again and again when you ask locals where to eat. It's family-run, it's been around long enough to build a real following, and it consistently ranks among the top-reviewed restaurants in Pinetop-Lakeside on sites like Tripadvisor and Yelp. If your group is craving Mexican food after a day at the cabin, this is a solid bet.

## What to Know About Los Corrales

Los Corrales is known for a few things in particular: large portions, good margaritas, and carne asada that regulars keep coming back for. It's not a fancy sit-down experience — it's a comfortable, family-oriented spot that's built its reputation the old-fashioned way, by feeding the same customers well for years.

One thing worth knowing ahead of time: it's popular. Locals will tell you there can be a wait at peak times, especially on weekend evenings when both visitors and residents are looking for a table. That's not a knock on the place — it's just a sign of how well-liked it is. If you're bringing a big group, it's worth calling ahead to see if they can accommodate you, or planning to arrive a little earlier than you might otherwise.

Listed hours are Monday through Friday, 11am to 9:30pm. Weekend hours aren't consistently listed, so if you're planning a Saturday or Sunday visit, it's worth calling the restaurant directly to confirm before you head over.

## Why It's a Good Stop for a Big Group

When you've got a crew of 15, 20, or more staying at the cabin, finding a restaurant that can handle a large party without a hassle matters. Los Corrales' reputation for generous portions makes it a practical choice for groups with a mix of appetites — from picky kids to hungry teenagers to adults who want a real plate of food after a day of hiking or lake time. And if a few people in your group want to unwind with a margarita while the rest of the table digs into carne asada, this is the kind of place set up for that.

It's also a nice change of pace from the American classics you tend to find in mountain towns. A big group vacation often means a few nights of grilling at the cabin, so working in a night out for Mexican food gives everyone something different to look forward to.

## Getting There from the Cabins

Pinetop is a short drive from Lakeside, so Los Corrales is easily reachable whether your group is staying at [Parkway Lodge](/parkway-lodge/) or the [Mohave Cabin with a Treehouse](/mohave-cabin-treehouse/). It's on White Mountain Boulevard, the main road running through Pinetop, so it's easy to find and easy to pair with other stops in town — shopping, groceries, or another meal out later in the week.

If you're building out a few restaurant nights for your stay, Los Corrales pairs well with other Pinetop favorites. [Charlie Clark's Steakhouse](/blog/charlie-clarks-steakhouse-pinetop/) is a good option if part of your group is in the mood for steak instead of Mexican food, and [Persnickety's Cafe](/blog/persnikkiys-cafe-pinetop/) is worth keeping in mind for a lighter breakfast or lunch earlier in your trip.

## A Few Practical Tips

Because it's a popular spot, plan around the wait rather than being surprised by it. Weeknights tend to be calmer than weekends, and going earlier in the evening usually beats the dinner rush. If your group is large — think eight or more — call ahead. Most family-run restaurants appreciate the heads-up, and it gives them a chance to either seat you together or let you know how long the wait might run.

Since weekend hours aren't clearly posted, a quick phone call before you leave the cabin can save you a wasted trip, especially if you're heading over on a Sunday.
