When you've got a cabin full of people, mornings have a rhythm of their own. Someone's making pancakes, someone's still asleep, and at least two or three people are wandering around looking for coffee that isn't instant. The Pinetop Coffee House & Roasting Company, at 1453 E White Mountain Blvd in Pinetop, is exactly the fix for that — a locally owned shop that roasts its own beans on-site, so what's in your cup actually comes from down the road rather than a warehouse somewhere else.

That's not a small detail for coffee people. A lot of shops buy their beans from a big regional roaster and just brew what shows up. Pinetop Coffee House does its own roasting, which means the coffee is fresher and the shop has real control over how it tastes. It's the kind of operation coffee drinkers notice and come back for.

## Locally Owned and Veteran-Operated

The shop is locally owned and veteran-operated, which fits the White Mountains pretty well — this is a region full of small, independently run places rather than chains, and the coffee shop is no exception. It's earned a following for it too: Pinetop Coffee House was named Best Coffee Shop in the greater White Mountains for 2022 and 2023, and Best Coffee Shop in Pinetop-Lakeside for 2023. That's back-to-back recognition, not a one-time fluke, and it lines up with what you'd expect from a shop that's serious about the actual coffee rather than just the concept of one.

## A Good, Easy Breakfast Stop

This isn't a sit-down restaurant, so don't plan a big group breakfast reservation around it. Think of it as the casual stop — coffee, something to eat, and you're back out the door. For a cabin trip, that's often exactly what you want: a quick errand into town rather than a whole outing. Send one person on a coffee run while everyone else finishes waking up, and come back with a round of drinks that are actually roasted with some care behind them.

If your group has a mix of coffee snobs and people who just want caffeine, this is a shop that can satisfy both. The snobs get fresh, locally roasted beans; everyone else gets a solid cup without having to think about it.

## Pairs Well with a Pinetop Morning

Pinetop-Lakeside has a handful of good breakfast and coffee spots, and it's worth treating them as a rotation rather than picking just one for the whole trip. If you want a sit-down cafe experience on a different morning, [Persnickety's Cafe](/blog/persnikkiys-cafe-pinetop/) is a cozy nearby option with breakfast, lunch, and its own good coffee. And if your group has golfers in it, a coffee run to Pinetop Coffee House is an easy way to fuel up before hitting one of the [Pinetop golf courses](/blog/pinetop-golf-courses/) — grab a cup on the way and you'll be caffeinated by the first tee.

## Getting There from the Cabins

Pinetop is close to both of our properties, so this is an easy stop whether you're staying at [Parkway Lodge](/parkway-lodge/) or the [Mohave Cabin with a Treehouse](/mohave-cabin-treehouse/). It's a short drive in either direction — close enough that "let's grab coffee" doesn't turn into a production. Groups staying at either cabin can swing by on the way to breakfast, on the way out for a hike, or just because the pot at the cabin ran dry before everyone was awake.

## Good to Know Before You Go

Coffee shop hours in a small mountain town can shift by season, and a locally roasted shop may sell through certain beans or drinks depending on the day, so it's worth calling ahead if you have your heart set on something specific. Otherwise, it's a low-stakes stop — pull in, order, and get back to the cabin before the coffee gets cold.
