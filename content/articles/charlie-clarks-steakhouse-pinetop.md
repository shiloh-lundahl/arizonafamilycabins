Charlie Clark's Steakhouse in Pinetop, Arizona is a genuine White Mountains institution, dating back to the 1930s. It's known for steaks, prime rib, and a historic bar and patio that have been part of the local scene for the better part of a century. When your group wants to make one dinner feel like an occasion, this is the spot.

Places this old don't survive on nostalgia alone. Charlie Clark's has stayed a fixture because it keeps delivering the kind of night out people remember.

## A Restaurant With Real History

Not many restaurants in Arizona can claim roots going back to the 1930s, and Charlie Clark's wears that history well. The historic bar is a big part of the appeal — it carries the weathered, lived-in character you only get from decades of serving locals, ranchers, tourists, and everyone in between. Grabbing a drink there before dinner is a little bit of White Mountains tradition.

That longevity tells you something. A restaurant doesn't stay open across generations without earning its place, and Charlie Clark's has earned it the hard way, one good dinner at a time.

## What It's Known For

Steaks and prime rib are the headliners here. This is a proper steakhouse, the kind of place you go when you want a real cut of beef and the ceremony that comes with it. The historic bar and patio round out the experience, giving you somewhere to settle in, unwind, and let dinner stretch out the way a good one should.

When you've got a large group in from a day outdoors and everyone's ready to clean up and go somewhere nice, this is the answer. It's the "special dinner out" of a White Mountains trip.

## Perfect for a Group Night Out

Traveling with a big crew changes how you think about dinner. Most nights you might cook at the cabin or grab something casual, but a trip usually deserves at least one real sit-down meal where everyone dresses up a notch. Charlie Clark's is built for that moment.

If your group is staying at [Parkway Lodge](/parkway-lodge/), which sleeps 27, or the [Mohave Cabin with a Treehouse](/mohave-cabin-treehouse/), which sleeps 33, this is the kind of place to gather everyone for one memorable dinner. For a group that size, call ahead so they can plan for your table — a little coordination makes a big-group dinner far smoother.

Charlie Clark's is in Pinetop, an easy drive from the cabins in Lakeside. That closeness makes it realistic to plan a proper group dinner out without a long haul home afterward. ## Good to Know Before You Go

For a special dinner, and especially for a big party, reservations or a call ahead are your friend. Hours at mountain restaurants can shift with the seasons, so it's worth confirming before you build the evening around it. And since this is the fancier night of the trip, it's a good excuse to trade the hiking boots for something a little nicer.
