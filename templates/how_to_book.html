Good to Know Before You Book

  • Two cabins: Parkway Lodge sleeps up to 27; Mohave Cabin with a Treehouse sleeps up to 33. Need more? Book both for up to 60 guests.
  • Deposit: A deposit holds your reservation — we'll walk you through it.
  • Minimum stay: Varies by season, especially summer and holiday weekends. Ask us when you reach out.
  • Peak season: Summer and holiday weekends book early, so the sooner you send your dates, the better.
  • Direct booking: Booking with us means no Airbnb or VRBO service fees.
